MODULES M1–M6 · THE ONLINE CLASSROOM

Most training platforms just play videos.

Prentice1 stops to teach. Every lesson pauses at the moments that matter, asks what you'd do next, and shows you the anatomy underneath.

  1. 01Video that stops to teach

    The lesson holds the frame at the moments that matter and asks what you'd do next. A required stop has to be answered before you move on.

  2. 02Every answer explained

    Right or wrong, every option tells you why, and Rewatch takes you back to the exact seconds that answer it.

  3. 03Peel the face in 3D

    Turn the model, peel it from skin to bone, and tap any structure for its notes.

  4. 04A coach that cites the moment

    Ask about what you're watching. The Lesson Coach answers only from the lesson, links the second it was said, and sends what it can't answer to faculty.

  5. 05Search every moment

    Find a phrase, a structure or a product across your lessons and open the video at the second it comes up.

  6. 06Laptop, iPad or phone

    Your place, your answers and the stop you were on are saved, so you pick up on any device where you left off.

THE INTENSIVE · MARCH 8–12, 2027 · CALIFORNIA

Learn on a real human face.

Diagrams can't teach depth. In the cadaver lab you work through every layer of the face, from fat pads and muscle to vessels and nerves, with Dr. Rivkin guiding.

LAYER BY LAYER

Know which plane you're in.

Skin, superficial fat, muscle, deep fat and bone: what runs through each layer and why it matters for every injection.

INDUSTRY-GRADE PRODUCT

Train with what practices use.

The toxins and fillers you will carry into a practice, not teaching substitutes.

SUPERVISED

Every injection watched.

Faculty correct your plane, depth and volume in the moment, on a live face — and what they observe you do is what your Readiness Standard records.
