THE INTENSIVE · MARCH 8–12, 2027 · CALIFORNIA

Learn on a real human face.

Diagrams can't teach depth. In the cadaver lab you work through every layer of the face, from fat pads and muscle to vessels and nerves, with Dr. Rivkin guiding.

LAYER BY LAYER

Know which plane you're in.

Skin, superficial fat, muscle, deep fat and bone: what runs through each layer and why it matters for every injection.

INDUSTRY-GRADE PRODUCT

Train with what practices use.

The toxins and fillers you will carry into a practice, not teaching substitutes.

SUPERVISED

Every injection watched.

Faculty correct your plane, depth and volume in the moment, on a live face — and what they observe you do is what your Readiness Standard records.

HOW ADMISSION WORKS
  1. 01Career Map callThirty minutes with an advisor before you apply: what your license allows, demand in your market, what a first role actually pays, and the time and money this takes.
  2. 02ApplyAbout seven minutes in one sitting: license, state, experience and goals. Your answers save as you go, so nothing is lost if you stop.
  3. 03Full application and interviewWe verify your license, then send a longer application — about 30 to 45 minutes on practice history, prior training and preferences — and invite shortlisted applicants to a recorded interview with a panel.
  4. 04OfferA decision within 5 business days of the interview, with tuition and financing terms. Your offer is held for 7 days.
  5. 05Reserve your seatA $1,000 refundable deposit holds your seat. Pay the balance or start a Cherry plan, then your pre-cohort work opens on January 3.
