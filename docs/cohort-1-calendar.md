# Cohort 1 calendar — derived

Every date published on prentice1.com comes from this table. It is derived, not
chosen: **Prentice Day is fixed at Saturday 13 March 2027** and every other
milestone is worked backwards from it using the intervals the six-week
curriculum specifies.

Decided by the owner, 2026-10-18, with decisions D1–D6 (below).

---

## The anchor and the arithmetic

Prentice Day is the one big networking event: graduation, the keynote and
employer panel, the pre-matched employer meetings and the cohort social, in one
day. The curriculum calls this stage the Summit (J25); Prentice1 calls it
Prentice Day, and there is only ever one such event per cohort.

Working backwards from it, the intervals the curriculum fixes are:

| Interval | Source |
|---|---|
| Graduation within 2 weeks of the intensive | 00 Spine J25 · 05 In Person & Graduation |
| Capstone stations 5–10 days after the intensive | 00 Spine J23–J24 |
| Intensive runs 5 consecutive days | 05 In Person & Graduation |
| Clearance decision exactly 7 days before the intensive (T−7) | 00 Spine J19 |
| Pre-arrival window T−12 to T−1, briefing at T−2 | 05 §1.6 |
| Six online Modules of 7 days each | 04 Online Learning & Assessment §2 |
| Exam and oral review on days 39–41 | 00 Spine J17–J18 |
| Pre-cohort runway days −14 to 0 | 03 Enrollment, Payment & Onboarding §8 |

Prentice Day lands 8 days after the intensive ends, which leaves Wed–Fri for the
capstone stations. That is tight but it holds, and it has an upside the
curriculum's own sequencing does not: **the Readiness Standard is awarded at
Prentice Day**, in front of the practices, rather than in the gap before it.

## Three things this calendar changed

**The cohort start moved from 25 January to 11 January.** The window the site
published, 25 Jan – 13 Mar, is 47 days. The journey needs 61. Six online
Modules, the exam, clearance, the pre-arrival window, a five-day intensive and
the capstone do not fit in 47 days, so with Prentice Day fixed the start had to
move two weeks earlier. Nothing else would have reconciled.

**The application deadline moved from 10 January 2027 to 27 November 2026.**
Under the derived calendar 10 January is day 0 — the day before teaching
starts. The admissions funnel behind it is a Career Map call, the Intake,
verification, a 30–45 minute Full Application, a recorded panel interview, a
decision within 5 business days and an offer held for 7 days; then the contract,
payment, account setup and a 14-day pre-cohort runway carrying Primer 4, the
120-term terminology set and a reflective piece. That is four to five weeks of
work after the last application arrives. 27 November is the latest date that
leaves the runway intact.

**Day numbers shift by one against the curriculum's.** The curriculum numbers
clearance as day 42 and the intensive as days 49–53. Anchoring the online weeks
Monday–Sunday — which every live session and weekly rhythm assumes — puts both
of those on a Sunday. Holding the intervals instead of the labels makes
clearance **day 43** and the intensive **days 50–54**. Every gap the curriculum
specifies is preserved; only the ordinals move. Docs 03, 05, 10 and 11 still
carry the older five-week dates (intensive days 35–39) and need reconciling
against this table.

## Open, and not settled by this table

- **Intensive venue.** The curriculum says California; the site says Los
  Angeles throughout, including its canonical URL, OG images and schema. The
  intensive is published here as California, matching the curriculum, and
  Prentice Day as Los Angeles, which is fixed. Confirm the venue and the two can
  be made to agree.
- **Weekly hours.** The Learner Journey says about 16 hours a week; the welcome
  video says 12–18. Published as 12–18 because it is the honest range and the
  narrower figure sits inside it.
- **Orientation on 6 January** is a recommendation, not a derivation. The
  runway's own window opens on 27 December, across the holidays.
