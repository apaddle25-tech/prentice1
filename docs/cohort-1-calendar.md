## The anchors and the arithmetic

Prentice Day is the one big networking event: graduation, the keynote and
employer panel, the pre-matched employer meetings and the cohort social, in one
day. The curriculum calls this stage the Summit (J25); Prentice1 calls it
Prentice Day, and there is only ever one such event per cohort. It sits
immediately after the intensive, so the cohort makes one trip, not two, and
walks into the room still warm from the chair.

Those two anchors fix the intensive at **Monday 8 to Friday 12 March**, and the

## What this calendar changed, and what it costs

**The cohort starts 18 January, not 25 January.** The window the site published,
25 Jan – 13 Mar, is 47 days. The journey needs 54: six online Modules, the
exam, clearance, the pre-arrival window and a five-day intensive do not fit in
47 days, so with Prentice Day fixed the start had to move a week earlier.

**The application deadline is 23 December 2026, not 10 January 2027.** Under
this calendar 10 January is day −8 — inside the pre-cohort runway, with the
whole funnel still to run behind it. 23 December is the last working day before
the holidays, and it is the latest date that still leaves: five business days
to a decision (published as Monday 4 January, so nobody is told on New Year's
Eve), the full seven-day offer window, enrolment and account setup, and the
orientation plus about 3 hours 20 minutes of pre-work before teaching starts.
Applications are assessed on arrival rather than held to the deadline, so
anyone who applies earlier enrols earlier and gets the whole runway.

**The runway no longer spans the holidays.** At a 25 January start the runway
fell on 27 December to 10 January and asked for 3 h 20 of pre-work plus a live
orientation across Christmas and New Year. It now runs 3 to 17 January, entirely
inside term time, at its specified 14 days. This was a real defect and it fixed
itself.

**The capstone has to move into the intensive week.** This is the one cost, and
it is a curriculum change, not a scheduling one. Doc 00 puts the capstone at
J23–J24, five to ten days after the intensive: a five-case portfolio, a 15-minute
practical station, a 15-minute complication station, a 20-minute blinded oral
and a 30-minute career review. With Prentice Day on the Saturday immediately
after the intensive there is no gap left to put it in. Either the stations run
inside the five days — Day 5 already carries a readiness review, and the
portfolio can be submitted before arrival — or the Readiness Standard is awarded
a week or two *after* the hiring event, which defeats the point of awarding it
at all. The site is written on the first reading. **Docs 00 and 05 need
amending to match.**

**Day numbers shift by one against the curriculum's.** The curriculum numbers
clearance as day 42 and the intensive as days 49–53. Anchoring the online weeks
Monday–Sunday — which every live session and weekly rhythm assumes — puts both
on a Sunday. Holding the intervals instead of the labels makes clearance
**day 43** and the intensive **days 50–54**. Every gap the curriculum specifies
is preserved; only the ordinals move. Docs 03, 05, 10 and 11 still carry the
older five-week dates (intensive days 35–39) and need reconciling against this
table.

## Amended 27 October 2026

The owner moved the exam and clearance so the exam no longer overlaps Module 6
week, on the condition that Prentice Day stays on its weekend. It does:

- **Foundations Exam and oral review: Mon 1 – Wed 3 Mar 2027** (days 43–45; was
  Thu 25 – Sat 27 Feb, which fell inside Module 6 week).
- **Clearance decision: Thu 4 Mar 2027** (day 46, T−4; was Mon 1 Mar, T−7).
- Unchanged: the pre-arrival window, the briefing on Sat 6 Mar, the intensive
  Mon 8 – Fri 12 Mar and **Prentice Day, Sat 13 Mar**.

The cost is three days between clearance and the briefing instead of five, so
travel booked on clearance has less notice. The curriculum's T−7 (00 Spine J19)
needs amending to match. The platform's calendar (`content/pilot/cohort-calendar.json`
in prentice1-platform, DL-054) carries the same dates.

The day-number paragraph above describes the calendar as derived on 18 October;
under this amendment clearance is day 46, not day 43.

## Open, and not settled by this table

- **Intensive venue.** The curriculum says California; the site says Los
  Angeles in its title, canonical URL, OG images and schema. The intensive is
  published as California, matching the curriculum, and Prentice Day as Los
  Angeles, which is fixed. Confirm the venue and the two can be made to agree —
  and now that the two sit on consecutive days, they had better be in the same
  city.
- **Weekly hours.** The Learner Journey says about 16 hours a week; the welcome
  video says 12–18. Published as 12–18 because it is the honest range and the
  narrower figure sits inside it.
- **Orientation on 14 January** is a recommendation, not a derivation. It is
  placed after the latest possible enrolment date so that every admitted
  learner can attend it live.
