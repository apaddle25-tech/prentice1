Privacy

What this website collects, why, how long we keep it, and how to see, correct or delete it. Written to be read rather than to be survived.

Applies to prentice1.com. The learning platform at app.prentice1.com has its own terms, which cover what is recorded while you study.

Browsing this site collects nothing

No cookies are set, no analytics are running, and nothing is stored in your browser. There is no banner to dismiss because there is nothing to consent to.

One exception, and it is not about you: every page asks app.prentice1.com whether the browser already holds a Prentice1 session, so the menu can show "Log in" or "Dashboard". The answer is a single yes or no. No name, no account, no identifier comes back, and nothing is recorded by asking.

If we start measuring traffic, this notice changes before the measuring does.

What the application form collects

If you apply at prentice1.com/apply, we collect exactly these:

  • Your name and email. To reply to you.
  • Your mobile number, if you choose to give it. Optional, and only used if you would rather we called.
  • Your license type, the state you are licensed in, and your years of clinical experience. Cohort 1 is open to licensed RNs, NPs, PA-Cs and MDs, and these are how we tell whether you are eligible.
  • What you want from the program, if you write it. Optional.

That is the whole list. The form does not ask for your license number, your date of birth, your address or any identity document, and it cannot: those fields do not exist on it.

If you are invited to continue, the full application asks for more, including verification details. You will be told what and why at that point, on that form — not here, and not retrospectively.

Who sees it

Prentice1 staff reviewing applications. That is all.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. Hiring practices do not receive your application. If you are accepted and later choose to be introduced to a practice at Prentice Day, we broker that introduction and you decide what is shared — and your phone number is never part of it.

How long it is kept

While your application is open, for as long as it takes to decide.

After a decision — whichever way it goes — your application is kept for 24 months and then deleted. The clock starts at the decision, not at the day you applied, so an application still being considered is never deleted out from under you.

You can ask us to delete it sooner. See below.

Companies that handle it on our behalf

  • GitHub Pages serves this website.
  • Render runs our application and the database your answers are stored in.
  • Resend sends the confirmation email and the notification to our team.

Each of them handles your information only to do that job. If you book a call with us, that is through Calendly, and what you give Calendly is covered by Calendly's own notice.

Your choices

Whoever and wherever you are, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you;
  • correct anything wrong;
  • delete it.

California residents have these rights under the CCPA, as amended by the CPRA, including the right to know what is collected and why and the right not to be treated differently for exercising them. We will not treat you differently, and asking will not affect an application. We have nothing to offer under the right to opt out of sale or sharing, because we do neither.

Write to privacy@prentice1.com. We will ask enough to be sure it is you, and no more.

Children

This program is for licensed clinicians. The site is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date below changes with it. A change that affects information already given to us will be told to you directly, not left here to be found.

Contact
